HOW HE FIGHTS
Range
Crawford adjusts his distance to whatever his opponent does. He starts patiently at long range and closes in once he has read the rhythm.
Defence
A calm, responsive guard and constant small movements make opponents miss, and each miss becomes the start of a counter.
Offence
Switching between orthodox and southpaw changes which hand leads and which angles are open, so punches arrive from places the opponent has not prepared for.

ADOPT THIS STYLE
01
Learn both stances
Shadow-box a round in your natural stance, then a round in the opposite one. Keep the weight balanced and the guard high in both.
02
Switch on the move
Practise changing the lead foot with a small hop between punches. Start slowly and keep the feet about shoulder-width apart.
03
Counter on purpose
Have a partner throw a slow jab, step off the line, and answer. Repeat in both stances, and practise with a coach when you can.
