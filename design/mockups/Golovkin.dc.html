GOLOVKIN
CALM PRESSURE
STANCE: ORTHODOX
STYLE IN ONE SENTENCE
Golovkin walks opponents down with a heavy jab and balanced footwork, cutting off the ring before he breaks them with body shots.
STIFF JAB CUT THE RING BODY SHOTS
HOW HE FIGHTS
RANGE
Golovkin stays within jab range and keeps stepping forward. Balanced steps shrink the ring, so there is rarely a place for the opponent to retreat to.
DEFENCE
A tight guard and a steady jab keep opponents at the end of his reach. He relies more on cutting off their exits than on dodging punches.
OFFENCE
The jab sets the range and opens the door for heavy hooks, with the left hook to the body wearing opponents down over the rounds.

ADOPT THIS STYLE
01
FIND THE JAB
Stand balanced with your weight even on both feet. Throw slow, controlled jabs for three rounds, snapping each one back.
02
WALK THEM DOWN
Mark a small ring with tape and shadow-box in it. Step to the side your imaginary opponent wants to escape to, without crossing your feet.
03
BREAK THE BODY
Add the left hook to the body behind the jab, then step out. Keep the rhythm steady rather than rushed, and practise with a coach when you can.
