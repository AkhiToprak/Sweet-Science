LOMACHENKO
FIND THE ANGLE
STANCE: SOUTHPAW · LAYOUT MIRRORED
STYLE IN ONE SENTENCE
Lomachenko turns every exchange into a geometry problem, stepping off the line and pivoting to punch from angles his opponent cannot see.
SOUTHPAW PIVOTS ANGLES
HOW HE FIGHTS
Range
Lomachenko rarely stays in front of his opponent. He steps off the centre line so each exchange happens at an angle where he can punch and they cannot.
Defence
His best defence is position. By turning and stepping away from the line of attack, punches miss before he needs to block or slip them.
Offence
Quick combinations arrive from unusual angles, several punches at a time, and he pivots out before the reply comes.

ADOPT THIS STYLE
01
Step off the line
Draw a line on the floor and stand on it. Take a small step to the side after each jab, so you never finish where you started.
02
Pivot
Plant the front foot and turn the body around it to face a new angle. Go slowly first, then add a punch as you turn.
03
Trace the triangle
Tape a triangle on the floor and move from corner to corner, throwing a short combination from each one. Practise with a coach when you can.
