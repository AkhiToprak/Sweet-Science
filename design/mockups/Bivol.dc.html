STYLE IN ONE SENTENCE
Bivol controls the range with a stiff jab and calm footwork, then scores in tidy, high-volume bursts without taking unnecessary risks.
STIFF JAB RANGE CONTROL COMPOSED
HOW HE FIGHTS
Range
Bivol manages distance with the jab and small, measured steps. He stays just outside his opponent's reach and steps in only to score.
Defence
He avoids trouble by never offering a clean target: a compact guard, shoulder turns, and a step out of range after each exchange.
Offence
Everything starts with the jab and flows into the right hand and short combinations, thrown in tidy bursts at a steady rate.

ADOPT THIS STYLE
01
Measure the distance
Stand an arm's length from a wall or a bag and find the point where your jab just reaches it. Repeat until the distance feels natural.
02
Step in, step out
Throw a jab, then take a small step back before resetting. Stay on the balls of the feet and keep the stance narrow.
03
Score and leave
Link a jab and a right hand, then pivot out of range. Aim for tidy, repeatable combinations rather than power, and practise with a coach when you can.
